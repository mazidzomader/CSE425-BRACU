## Topic-wise Reading List

### Cost Functions

- *Deep Learning* — Chapter 3: Neural Network Fundamentals (Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville)
- Coursera — **Machine Learning** by Andrew Ng: cost functions and practical applications
- *A Gentle Introduction to Optimization* — Suvrit Sra: mathematical perspective on cost functions and optimization

### Optimization Algorithms

- *Deep Learning* — Chapter 8: optimization methods (Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville)
- Blog post — **"An overview of gradient descent optimization algorithms"** by Sebastian Ruder
- Stanford **CS231n**: optimization algorithms in the context of neural networks

### Gradient Descent

- *Deep Learning* — Chapter 8: gradient descent and practical implementation for deep learning
- *An Introduction to Optimization* — Chong and Żak: comprehensive treatment of gradient descent

### Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD)

- *Deep Learning* — Chapter 8: SGD and its variants for training deep neural networks
- *Introduction to Artificial Neural Networks and Deep Learning* — Sandro Skansi: dedicated chapter on SGD
- Coursera — **Machine Learning** by Andrew Ng: accessible coverage of SGD
